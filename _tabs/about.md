Hey, I'm Anthony — welcome to my corner of the internet.

## What I do

I work as a Technology Technician , where I keep my organizations worth of devices, networks, and people running smoothly. Before that, I spent a few years at Apple , which taught me two things: how to actually fix things, and how to explain the fix to someone who just wants their laptop to work again. I hold an A.S. in Computer Networking Administration and Security Management, and I'm currently working through Security+ as a stepping stone toward where I really want to end up: **detection engineering**.

## What I'm into

Outside of the day job, I'm the kind of person who never really clocks out of "tinkering mode." A few things that eat up my free time:

- **Gaming** — my go-to way to unwind (and occasionally rage-quit).
- **Building and repairing PCs** — there's something satisfying about a clean build and a system that actually performs the way it should.
- **Self-hosting** — I'm always hunting for the next self-hosted tool to replace a service I'm paying someone else for. If I can run it myself, I probably will.
- **Podcasting** - I host a podcast all about the representation of technology in film. Check it out [Celluloid Circuits](https://rss.com/podcasts/celluloid-circuits/)

## What I'm building

At home I run a growing **home lab** that doubles as a **security lab** — a sandbox where I can break things on purpose, poke at network setups, and practice the skills that don't show up at my day job. It's equal parts hobby and long-term career investment: every VLAN I misconfigure and fix teaches me something that'll matter when I'm doing detection engineering for real.

## Where I'm headed

Short-term, that's Security+. Long-term, I'm working toward a role in **detection engineering** — building and tuning the systems that catch the stuff attackers don't want you to see. Everything I'm doing right now, from the day job to the home lab, is pointed at that goal.

Thanks for stopping by. Feel free to look around.
